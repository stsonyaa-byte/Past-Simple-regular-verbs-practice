🔥 Warm-up: How to build Past Simple

➕ PositiveSubject + verb-edShe played football.
➖ NegativeSubject + didn’t + verbShe didn’t play football.
❓ QuestionDid + subject + verb?Did she play football?
Yes, she did. / No, she didn’t.
Spelling: like → liked · play → played · cry → cried · stop → stopped
Remember: after didn’t and Did the verb has NO -ed!
